# Decisions log

Deviations/extensions beyond docs/implementation, per 00-overview §Decisions log.

- **2026-09-01** — Settings (theme, device id, volumes, sidebar) persist in `localStorage` (`ks.settings.v1`), not the Dexie `settings` table. Alternatives: Dexie as 02 lists. Reason: synchronous boot (no theme flash) and 02 itself allows localStorage for tiny prefs; Dexie remains the store for progress data. Extends 02 §Settings.
- **2026-09-01** — Playwright uses the sandbox's pinned Chromium via `launchOptions.executablePath` when `/opt/pw-browsers/chromium` exists and not in CI. Extends 01 §Testing.
- **2026-09-02** — New `Target` kind `'chord-any'` (multiple accepted chords per target, optional lone-bass-root answers) carries harmonization (`acceptAlternatives`) and `ear-progression`. Wait-mode only; the tempo matcher rejects it. Alternatives: overload `'set'` with an alternatives list. Reason: the accept-list semantics (any inversion, any listed chord, root-only shortcut) diverge enough from exact-set matching that a distinct kind keeps both matchers simple. Extends 04 §Targets.
- **2026-09-02** — Harmonization "functional match" uses the classic function groups (tonic {I,vi,iii}, subdominant {IV,ii}, dominant {V,vii°}) against the bar's canonical chord; melody notes are always chord tones of the canonical progression. 06 s3.u6 left the exact accept rule open. Extends 06.
- **2026-09-02** — Voice-leading scoring is applied inside the matchers at completion (the generator ships reference voicings on `ExerciseInstance.voiceLeading`); the vl blend replaces the base score exactly per 04 (0.5/0.3/0.2) with no extra-note penalty re-applied. Missed chords are bridged out of both movement sums so they punish pitch accuracy only.
- **2026-09-02** — `progression-play`/`chart-play` `style:'brokenLH'` renders root·fifth·octave·fifth note targets per bar (LH around C2), plus an exact-voicing RH set target on the bar line when `hand:'both'` (exact octaves so LH notes can't cross-match the chord).
- **2026-09-02** — Placement (05 §Welcome step 4) runs `/lesson/s{0,1,2}.cp?placement=1` back-to-back; passing a checkpoint marks its whole stage passed (07 §Gates) with the checkpoint's score as the tracked atoms' initial `bestScore`. A failed placement item offers "Start my path here"; exiting the chain has the same effect.
- **2026-09-02** — Create-step takes are saved to replays under a synthetic `improv-sandbox` exercise with a nominal all-pass result (create is never scored, 07 invariant 3); `stars: 0` marks them as unrated.
- **2026-09-02** — Review nodes on the Path launch a review-only workout (same builder as the 5-minute workout) rather than a bespoke session.
- **2026-09-02** — The ear-preview input gate (runStore) blocks only note-ons; note-offs always reach the wait matcher, otherwise its held-note set goes stale after a target advance (found via the s3.u6 e2e).

## Phase 7

- **2026-09-02** — 07 §Ratings says "floor: highest level band with ≥2 tracked atoms", which reads as a floor but cannot be one (it would pin the level to the hardest material learned). Implemented as a **clamp**: the level is bounded to the range of levels where a challenge is actually constructible — at least 2 tracked, drillable atoms inside the ±5 band. This matches the paragraph above it ("the rating can't exceed what the path has taught"), gives demotion a real floor at the bottom of that range, and makes a challenge always buildable. Extends 07.
- **2026-09-02** — A fresh strand starts at the **bottom of its supported range**, not level 1: with only hard material tracked, level 1's band would be empty and no challenge could be generated.
- **2026-09-02** — `atoms.ts` gained drills for `spell:`, `keysig:`, `ear:`, `prog:`, `prog-smooth:` and `song:` atoms. Before this only note/fivefinger/scale/chord atoms were drillable, which meant the theory and ear strands had **zero** reviewable or ratable atoms — 07's review and rating designs both assume every tracked atom can be drilled. `keysig:*` drills as a roman-numeral card in that key (you cannot answer it without knowing the signature); the choice-mode card 06 imagines is still deferred.
- **2026-09-02** — Badges are computed purely from Dexie on every read (07: "all computed from Dexie, no new state"). The only stored badge state is the "already celebrated" snapshot used to fire the toast once.
- **2026-09-02** — `getRecap()` writes its computed recap to `meta`, so it is **not** liveQuery-safe. Split into `readRecap()` (read-only, for `useLiveQuery`) and `getRecap()` (computes once per week, called from an effect) — the same split `readTodaySession`/`getTodaySession` already used.
- **2026-09-02** — Passing a checkpoint now calls `trackAtoms` for stage units that are _already_ marked passed, instead of skipping them. Without this, a unit marked passed by any route that bypassed `completeUnit` kept its atoms untracked forever, so they were never reviewed or available to a rating.
- **2026-09-02** — `keysig:c:major` added to s5.u6. The `circle-complete` badge requires all 12 key signatures and the curriculum only defined 11 — C, the one signature with nothing in it, was missing.
- **2026-09-02** — PWA icons are generated by `scripts/make-icons.mjs`, a ~120-line rasterizer for the app mark, rather than committed binaries from an image toolchain. The mark is only rounded rectangles, so the renderer is a scanline fill plus `node:zlib`; regenerating needs no dependency.

## Phase 8

- **2026-09-02** — 08 introduces Tone.js for improv backing loops. **Not adopted.** The app already has a sample-accurate beat scheduler driving the metronome, and two transports sharing one AudioContext drift against each other. `audio/backing.ts` rides the existing `onBeat` scheduler instead, which also keeps ~200 KB out of the bundle. Extends 08 §Phase 8.3.
- **2026-09-02** — Comp patterns are expressed as `style` values on `progression-play`/`chart-play` (with a `voicing` and `swing` param) rather than a separate generator, per 06's note that "comp patterns are beat-grid exercise variants of progression-play". `engine/comp.ts` owns the voicing and pattern tables.
- **2026-09-02** — The notation strand's atoms (`read:staff:*`) are **not** on any unit's concept list; they are registered directly and tracked only when the learner turns the strand on in Settings. 05 lists reading as optional, and putting read units in the curriculum would force every learner through them. `read:symbols:full` stays core curriculum and drills as chord-symbol spelling, since that is what it actually tests.
- **2026-09-02** — `--accent-text` split out from `--accent`. The accent was used both as a fill and as link/eyebrow text; at 3.41:1 on the dark surface it failed WCAG AA as text. The fill keeps its vividness, the text tint clears AA in both themes. `--text-3` was also darkened (light) and lightened (dark) — it failed AA on every surface in both themes. Found by the axe-core scan; now guarded at source by `scripts/check-contrast.mjs`.
- **2026-09-02** — Locked song cards use `aria-disabled` rather than `disabled`, so they stay in the tab order. With every song locked (a fresh profile) the scrolling `<main>` contained no focusable element at all, which made the screen unreachable by keyboard.
- **2026-09-02** — `chunkSizeWarningLimit` raised to 1200 KB because VexFlow is a deliberately lazy 1.1 MB chunk. The real guard is `scripts/check-bundle.mjs`, which enforces 08's "< 450 KB gz before samples/vexflow chunks" against the eager bundle and warns if vexflow ever stops being its own chunk.
